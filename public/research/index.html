PET-MOD-01 · Modular pet travel

BUY · 86

Setup: Fragmented pet travel accessories; weak bundling among top listings. Edge: Modular system raises AOV and supports content-led differentiation. Economics (base): ~$12 landed, ~$35 ASP, mid-teens ACOS path to low-teens with organic mix. Kill switches: Contribution <$6/unit after ads; tariff stack >+12% COGS without price power. Sizing: $40–50k first wave after pilot sell-through confirmation.

HOME-PREP-02 · Pro kitchen tool

BUY · 81

Setup: Narrow professional niche with willingness to pay; mass listings under-serve durability. Edge: Spec-led product + pro social proof; less CPC war than broad “gadget” keywords. Economics: Higher ASP supports fees; returns risk managed via QC and packaging. Kill switches: Review velocity stall past month 4; copycat flood from same factory map. Sizing: $25–35k; protect margin with controlled promo, not perpetual coupons.

SUP-REF-03 · Consumable refill

WATCH · 72

Setup: Repeat purchase economics are attractive if subscribe/attach works. Open questions: Customer acquisition cost for first unit; whether Amazon sub programs or off-Amazon retention is required. Why not BUY yet: Base clears hurdles only if repeat rate hits assumptions; we want 90 days of cohort data on a pilot. Next step: Small PO, measure reorder and organic share before scaling capital.

OUT-SEA-04 · Seasonal outdoor

HOLD · 64

Setup: Strong peak demand, weak off-season liquidity. Problem: Capital intensity + storage risk; miss the window and you fund the warehouse. Posture: Do not initiate new capital. Existing exposure should prioritize sell-through and avoid deep pre-buys without firm demand signals. Revisit: Only with pre-orders, tighter lead times, or a non-seasonal attach product.
