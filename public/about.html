The desk

About Big Sky ROI

A Wall Street–grade investment research desk for Amazon product capital allocation—built for operators who underwrite inventory like a portfolio.

What we are

Big Sky ROI is a product-research desk, not a blog of seller tips. We apply institutional tools—conviction scores, risk-adjusted ROI, position sizing, and written desk notes—to private-label, wholesale, and brand-acquisition-style bets on Amazon.

Inventory is capital. A purchase order is a capital call. Advertising is a tax on discovery. The permanent product here is judgment: whether a dollar of inventory capital clears its hurdle after the full fee and ad stack, and how that path breaks.

What you will find

  • A conviction board of illustrative product theses.
  • A seven-factor methodology and capital hurdles.
  • An interactive capital model for underwriting first-order inventory.
  • A research book of worked examples—economics, kill switches, sizing posture.

What we are not

We are not a registered investment adviser. We do not publish securities recommendations. Scores and models are educational research composites, not tickets and not guarantees. If an idea only works at heroic ACOS assumptions, it is not an idea—it is a hope. Hope is not a position.
