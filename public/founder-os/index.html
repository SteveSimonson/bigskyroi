How Founder OS gets a business plan together

A modern founder plan is not a single essay. It is a stack of instruments that stay synchronized. Founder OS is designed around that stack:

  1. Company job & offer Name who pays, for what, and why you win. Vague missions do not underwrite cash.
  2. Living financial forecast Revenue, costs, cash, and runway in one model—so “we’ll grow 10x” meets arithmetic.
  3. Capital & risk rules For product and inventory bets, pair with Big Sky ROI hurdles: cycle ROI, net margin floor, position size, kill switches.
  4. Story & brand spine Capture the narrative and brand system once so every hire and campaign reuses the same spine.
  5. Decision cadence Weekly wins, bottleneck breaks, advisor votes—so the plan updates when reality does.
